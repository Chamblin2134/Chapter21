# Avery Institute for Integrative Recovery — Design Ground Truth

The supplied HTML from the other agent is the visual and content ground truth for this continuation. Preserve its dark editorial treatment, warm gold accent system, Cormorant Garamond display typography, Manrope body typography, fixed institutional header, long-form section rhythm, and interactive resource/account surfaces. Any implementation decision should ask: **Does this preserve the calm, serious, accountable character of the institute?**

## Design Movement

Dark editorial institutional design with restrained luxury cues: archival black, warm brass, fine rules, generous vertical pacing, and a research-oriented reading experience.

## Core Principles

1. **Clarity before decoration:** information architecture, section labels, and readable line lengths carry the experience.
2. **Warm authority:** brass-gold accents signal care and competence without becoming ornamental or loud.
3. **Whole-person pacing:** long-form content gets room to breathe through deliberate whitespace and staggered sections.
4. **Accountability in the interface:** disclaimers, status messaging, focus states, and empty states are treated as first-class content.

## Color Philosophy

Near-black surfaces create a quiet, protected environment for serious recovery education. Bone-white text avoids the harshness of pure white, while a single warm brass range gives the institute an ownable signature and makes actions, labels, and emphasis discoverable.

## Layout Paradigm

A vertically unfolding editorial narrative rather than a conventional marketing grid. The page uses a fixed masthead, an asymmetric hero, horizontal statement band, concise service columns, and long-form chapters that alternate density and surface tone.

## Signature Elements

- Hairline brass rules and compact uppercase section labels.
- Cormorant Garamond headlines with selective brass emphasis.
- Layered charcoal surfaces and restrained borders that make cards feel archival rather than app-like.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should be direct, calm, and explicit. Clickable cards reveal context; tabs reveal one clear panel at a time; modal readers keep the user anchored; disabled or planned features explain their status rather than pretending to be live.

## Animation

Use short ease-out transitions for hover, focus, and panel changes. Keep movement subtle: slight elevation, border warming, and opacity transitions are preferred over large transforms. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System

Display: Cormorant Garamond, 500–700, for the institutional wordmark and editorial headings. Body: Manrope, 300–600, for navigation, labels, paragraphs, controls, and utility text. Uppercase labels use tracked Manrope at compact sizes; long-form copy stays at comfortable line height.

## Brand Essence

A serious, whole-person recovery education institute for people seeking practical understanding, professional development, and accountable pathways—not slogans. Personality: **grounded, humane, exacting**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and reflective. CTAs are specific and low-pressure. Microcopy is transparent about what is planned, proposed, or not yet active.

Example lines:

- “Understand the pattern. Protect the progress.”
- “A credential must mean something.”

## Wordmark & Logo

Use a typographic wordmark paired with a compact abstract mark: an open, nested line form suggesting pattern, progress, and purpose. The mark should be usable independently in the masthead and favicon, without relying on text.

## Signature Brand Color

Warm brass **#CC8C43**, used sparingly for headings, rules, labels, focus states, and primary actions.
