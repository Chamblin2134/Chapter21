- One-PDF upload automatically fills an editable title, detailed description, Store category, recovery topic, format, page count, keywords, and suggested price.
- Saved resources display six at a time with previous, next, and numbered page controls.
- Existing publishing, Supabase storage, protected previews, bundles, Store display, cart, Stripe checkout, and secure downloads remain in place.

## Chapter 21 signup gift

- Administrator can turn the prompt on or off and choose the exact free packet.
- Visitors sign up or sign in before receiving a private, time-limited download link.
- The email-updates checkbox is optional and does not affect packet access.
- Administrator can view requests six at a time and export a CSV containing only contacts who selected email consent.

## Reusable promotion campaigns

- Create unlimited custom campaigns; optional Summer, Fall, Halloween, Christmas, and New Year styles are shortcuts only.
- Choose 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, or any custom discount from 1% through 100%.
- Apply a campaign to all PDFs, one PDF, one bundle, coaching, coaching plus one PDF, or the whole Store and coaching area.
- Customize the campaign name, visitor heading, message, colors, optional image, button label, and destination.
- Preview, save, edit, duplicate, activate/schedule, deactivate, and delete campaigns.
- Only one campaign can be armed at a time; inactive campaigns stay saved for reuse.
- Optional start and end times make a campaign begin and expire automatically.
- Popup choices are once per campaign, once each day, or once per new browsing visit.
- The current offer appears as a website-entry popup, Store banner, eligible PDF price, and selected coaching badge.
- Checkout recalculates eligibility, schedule, and price on the server before opening Stripe.
- Each campaign reports deduplicated popup views, clicks, attributed signups, and verified purchases.
